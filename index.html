Yes. This is a monthly service, not a 12-month lock-in. If you're not seeing results, you can cancel. Asset ownership on cancellation is set out clearly in the service agreement — no surprises, no small print.

Home improvement businesses — joinery, flooring, remodelling, renovation, landscaping, roofing, and general building trades. The kind of business where a missed call or a slow reply directly costs jobs. If that's you, this system was built for you.
